1. Website Review and Fundraising Ideas

1(a) What helps and what hurts donations on the new site

I fetched and reviewed the preview pages on October 13, 2026: home, donate, meals, maas-place, blooming-star-village, get-involved, volunteer, and contact. I also read the donate page code.

What already helps

  • Clear impact math: $5 = one hot meal, $25 = a senior meal pack, $100 = a full delivery run for 20 people.
  • Strong trust signals: 501(c)(3), EIN shown, "secure payment via Stripe," "100% of donations fund meals."
  • One-time / Monthly toggle, tribute ("in honor of") giving, an FAQ, "Other ways to give," and a thank-you to named partners (Al Markaz Restaurant, Dare 2 Inc, Texas Wholesale, Popeyes Chicken, Chef Chen).
  • The Maa's Place page links to donate.html?program=maas-place, and the code passes the program to Stripe as client_reference_id, so those gifts can be tracked by program.
  • The Meals page tells Maa's story and has a real weekly video. That is the best fundraising asset on the site.

What hurts (and the fix)

How each main campaign works (step by step)

Maa's Circle (monthly giving). 1) Fix the monthly links (Issue 1). 2) Name the levels: $10 = 2 meals a month, $25 = 5 meals, $50 = 10 meals, $100 = Sponsor-a-Senior. 3) Ask current one-time donors first (letter template, Section 3). 4) Show a "Maa's Circle" line in every social Post 3 (Donation Impact). 5) Send members a monthly 60-second video update. Goal: 40 monthly donors by Dec 31, 2026, and 100 by Oct 2027 [confirm].

Sponsor-a-Senior / Sponsor-a-Meal. $25 feeds one senior family for a week (about 5 meals). $100 a month sponsors one senior family. A business can "Sponsor a Delivery Run" ($100 = hot meals for 20 people) or fund a tenth of a Saturday ($1,100 = 220 meals). No names of seniors are shared. Donors get a first-name-only or anonymous story, with consent.

Ramadan (about Feb 8 to Mar 9, 2027, by moon sighting). Faith communities are welcome as funders. Run it through partner mosques and community groups, using their channels: "Share a Meal: $5 feeds a neighbor, $500 sponsors 100 meals." Diwali (Sun Nov 8, 2026) and Thanksgiving (Thu Nov 26, 2026): "Share a Plate." Partner temples and businesses run a food or fund drive. Volunteers collect at events. Use Sponsor-a-Senior as the ask.

Maa's Day (Mother's Day, Sun May 9, 2027). This is the signature campaign. "Honor your mother the way Maa would: by feeding someone." Tribute gifts send an e-card to the mother being honored (the tribute form already exists). It runs Apr 19 to May 9 and leads into the Community Dinner.

Love All, Serve All Community Dinner (May 2027). A simple, home-style dinner, not a black-tie gala. 150 to 250 guests. Tickets [$] with a quid pro quo disclosure printed on them. Table sponsors, a 5-minute story, one live ask ("Fund a Saturday"), and an optional raffle (rules in Section 5). Budget $5,000 to $15,000. Aim for 3 times the cost or better.

Peer-to-peer and birthday fundraisers. Volunteers and donors set up personal pages ("For my birthday, help me serve 200 meals"). Use your CRM's or Givebutter/Zeffy's free peer-to-peer pages, or the North Texas Giving Day page (it supports fundraisers). Give each fundraiser a one-page kit with a photo, 3 captions, and the QR code.

Restaurant give-back nights. Start with existing partners (Al Markaz, the Popeyes franchise partner, Chef Chen). Ask for 15% to 25% of the night's sales or a fixed gift. Chipotle gives 25% of qualifying sales (applications reopen March 2027). Hold one every other month.

2. Other Funding Sources

2(a) Recommendations

  1. Start with food, not just cash. A food bank partnership (North Texas Food Bank for Dallas County clients, or Tarrant Area Food Bank for Denton County) can cut the cost per meal. NTFB reviews applications in January, April, July, and October.
  2. Apply now to quick, small corporate grants. Walmart Spark Good local grants ($250 to $5,000) are open until Nov 30, 2026. Kroger, Albertsons/Tom Thumb, H-E-B, and Sprouts follow.
  3. Build relationships with 4 or 5 foundations that take open applications: the Dallas Foundation (summer cycle), Meadows, Moody, and Rees-Jones (BSV, once it opens). Several large funders are invitation only (CFT, TI, Toyota, AT&T). For those, the route is relationships, not applications.
  4. Use the local network: Carrollton-Farmers Branch Rotary, faith communities, restaurants, and employers with matching gifts.
  5. Be honest about eligibility. Hoblitzelle needs an audit and a budget over $250,000. Crystal Charity Ball needs 3 years of serving children. Some Dallas Foundation cycles need a budget over $500,000. Don't spend time there until you qualify.

4(e) Grant management process

  1. Find (Assistant): keep the Funders tab current. Check deadlines on the 1st of each month.
  2. Decide (Aslam): go/no-go within 1 week of a match. Ask: do we fit, and is the effort worth the likely award?

  4. Draft (Assistant), review and submit (Aslam). Aim to finish 5 business days before the deadline.
  5. Track (Staff): status in the Funders tab: Researching / Drafting / Submitted / Awarded / Declined / Report due.
  6. Award (Aslam): sign the agreement. Put every report date and restriction in the calendar on the same day. Tag spending in QuickBooks by grant.
  7. Report (Assistant drafts, Aslam approves): send 2 weeks before the due date, with numbers, a story, and photos.
  8. Renew (Aslam): thank the funder 3 times a year. Ask about renewal 3 months before the grant ends.
  9. Declined? Ask for feedback politely and note when you can reapply.

5(e) Rules: raffles, events, donor privacy (Texas + IRS)

Texas Charitable Raffle Enabling Act (Occupations Code Ch. 2002). Verified with the Texas Attorney General.

Rule Detail
Who A 501(c) nonprofit that has existed at least 3 years (Maa's Inspiration likely qualifies at 5+ years; confirm the incorporation date)
How many Up to 4 raffles per calendar year. The brief said 2; the law changed from 2 to 4 in June 2021 (HB 2757).
Prizes No cash prizes (prepaid gift cards are allowed). A purchased prize can be worth up to $75,000. Donated prizes have no limit. You must own the prize or post a bond before the drawing.
Selling No statewide promotion or ticket sales. The AG says this is generally read to include online sales, so sell paper tickets in person. You can mention it on your own website and to existing supporters.
People No paid organizers or ticket sellers (volunteers only)
Ticket must show Org name · org (or officer) address · ticket price · description of each prize over $10 · drawing date
Not allowed Casino nights and poker nights (no nonprofit exception in Texas)
IRS Tickets are not deductible. A W-2G is required if the prize minus the ticket price is at least $2,000 (2026 threshold) and at least 300 times the ticket price. Withhold 24% if it is over $5,000. Confirm with the CPA.

Events - Print the quid pro quo disclosure on dinner tickets: "$[price] ticket includes dinner valued at $[value]; $[difference] is tax-deductible." - Texas sales tax: a qualifying 501(c)(3) can hold two one-day tax-free sales or auctions per calendar year. This needs Texas exemption status (Form AP-204). Confirm you have it. - Alcohol, food permits, venue insurance: confirm with the venue and city (Unverified here; most community dinners are alcohol-free, which keeps it simple).

Donor privacy - Adopt a one-paragraph donor privacy policy: "We never sell, rent, or share donor information." Put it on the donate page. - Keep donor data in the CRM (not personal Gmail or phones). Give access only to people who need it. - Card data stays with Stripe; never write card numbers down. - Honor "anonymous" and "do not contact" flags. Don't name donors publicly without permission. - Form 990 Schedule B donor names are not part of the public copy for 501(c)(3)s. Still, treat the list as confidential.

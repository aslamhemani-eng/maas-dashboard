Uploading the new website to GoDaddy

  1. Sign in at godaddy.com → My Products → Web Hosting → cPanel Admin.
  2. Open File Manager → go to public_html.
  3. Back up first: select all → Compress → download the zip.
  4. Click Upload and choose maasinspiration_upload.zip (ask Grok Bot to send it to you).
  5. Right-click the zip → Extract into public_html → allow it to replace files.
  6. Open maasinspiration.org, refresh, and check Home, Donate, and Volunteer.

The website is frozen. Nothing changes until you ask.

📲 Operational App: plan

One place for staff and volunteers to run the weekly work. Based on Aslam's mockups, App Breakdown sheet and template (Oct 13).

What it will do

  • Dashboard: this week at a glance: meal deliveries, sandwich count, volunteers checked in, low supplies, approvals.
  • Menu & vendors: a suggested weekly menu from our dish list; edit, approve, and send to vendors.
  • Street handout: set the weekly sandwich count and send it to the store.
  • Inventory: supplies on hand vs. ideal; weekly use auto-deducted; "reorder soon" alerts.
  • Volunteers & routes: admin roles, driver routes and delivery places (shelters), school hours.
  • Check-in kiosk: volunteers check in for shifts; hours are tracked.
  • Access: three levels: Owner, Admin, Volunteer.

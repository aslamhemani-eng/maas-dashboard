Questions for Aslam

  1. Numbers: What is the annual operating budget [$], the amount raised last year [$], and the number of donors [#]? What 12-month goal [$] do you want?
  2. Stripe: Are the 7 current Payment Links one-time or recurring? Who owns the Stripe account? May we apply for the nonprofit rate?
  3. Meal cost: Does "$5 per meal" include donated food? About how much of the Saturday food is bought vs. donated? (At $5, 2,200 meals is about $11,000 per Saturday.)
  4. The 100% claim: "100% of donations fund meals, no salaries" is confirmed for Maa's Meals. How should we word it for Maa's Place and BSV, which will have rent and other costs? Who covers costs like rent and Stripe fees today?
  5. Counties: Are 1445 MacArthur Dr and 2700 Old Denton Rd in Dallas or Denton County? Where do most clients live? (This decides NTFB vs. TAFB, Dallas AAA vs. NCTCOG, and Dallas Foundation eligibility.)
  6. Maa's Place opening date and opening budget? BSV opening date, staffing, and whether you have checked Texas child-care licensing rules for a day program for children 5 to 17 (Unverified here; check with Texas HHS Child Care Regulation)?
  7. Board: Who is on the board [names]? Is anyone willing to chair fundraising?
  8. Audit and 990: Do you have a recent Form 990 and financial statements? Any audit? (Needed for most foundations.)
  9. Texas sales-tax exemption: Has Maa's Inspiration filed Form AP-204 with the Texas Comptroller?
  10. Incorporation date (for the 3-year raffle rule)?
  11. Faith and community partners: Which 10 mosques, temples, churches, and community groups should be invited to partner?
  12. Businesses: Which 20 local businesses or restaurants should get the sponsor sheet, and may Aslam make those asks?
  13. Community Dinner: OK to plan for May 2027 (Maa's Day week)? Any venue in mind?
  14. Mailing address for checks, and which email should receive donor forms instead of the personal Gmail?
  15. Major gift level: Is $1,000 a year the right "major donor" line for you?
  16. Maa's memory: Is there a date (birthday or anniversary) you'd like to honor with a yearly "Maa's Day of Service"?

Uploading the new website to GoDaddy

  1. Sign in at godaddy.com → My Products → Web Hosting → cPanel Admin.
  2. Open File Manager → go to public_html.
  3. Back up first: select all → Compress → download the zip.
  4. Click Upload and choose maasinspiration_upload.zip (ask Grok Bot to send it to you).
  5. Right-click the zip → Extract into public_html → allow it to replace files.
  6. Open maasinspiration.org, refresh, and check Home, Donate, and Volunteer.

The website is frozen. Nothing changes until you ask.
